Demo harness · remove on copy Specification §01–§21

Everything marked remove-on-copy — this note, the draft inspector at the foot, and the four tweaks under Demo harness — exists to make the contract visible. Committing is a timer standing in for the routing-revision call; the comment above it says what to replace. The four tweaks under Pattern properties are not harness: surface, dismissalPolicy, cancelWhenDirty and postCommitDisposition are the §05 policies an adopting team must declare.

Open an operation and change a figure: the routing table behind keeps reading revision 6 until a commit succeeds, and Discard changes puts nothing back because nothing was written. Press Escape or click the scrim while dirty to meet the dismissalPolicy. Put OP-40 on WC-VMC-02, or a scrap allowance above 2% with a check every 100 pieces, for an invalid draft that stays editable. Turn on Routing service refuses the commit and save — every field survives with a message that says the routing did not change. Turn on Tool room revises the routing for a commit that fails because the draft was opened against an older revision, and Operation removed from the routing for a subject withdrawn mid-edit. Try opening a second operation while one is open: the refusal is a sentence and focus goes back to the open draft, not a dead button. The drawer is deliberately non-modal, so the routing table beside it stays readable and operable — which is what makes that refusal, and the unchanged committed figures, visible while a draft is open. The dialog surface is modal, and is where scrim dismissal is exercised.
