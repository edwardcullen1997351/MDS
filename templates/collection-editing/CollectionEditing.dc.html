Demo harness · remove on copy Specification §01–§21

Everything marked remove-on-copy — this note, the draft inspector below the plan, and the four tweaks under Demo harness — exists to make the contract visible. Saving is a timer standing in for the plan-commit call; the comment above it says what to replace. The four tweaks under Pattern properties are not harness: concurrency, entry, staleDraftPolicy and postSaveDisposition are the §05 policies an adopting team must declare.

Edit a slot: the committed row is never touched until the save succeeds, and Cancel puts the committed figures back. Type a quantity that is not a multiple of the pack size, or one above what the chosen shift and manning can make, for an invalid draft that stays editable. Turn on Plan commit fails and save — the draft survives with a message that says nothing was committed. Turn on Maintenance revises the slot you are editing for a stale draft, and Maintenance withdraws the slot for an item removed mid-edit. Filter to another press while a draft is open to see it kept and reported rather than dropped.
