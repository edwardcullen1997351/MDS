Selection-driven detail

Specification §01–§21Open standalonePatterns index

Live, on Meridian's own components: the press-shop tool crib at the Chakan plant (PL-04), where a clerk reads a die's tool life before handing it to a press. The crib row carries the current selection as a real radio; the stroke count, inspection history and refurbishment spend are read from MES into the panel beside it, and selecting is never navigation — sort the strokes column and the selection stays on the die. Issuing or grinding a die takes it out of the crib, which is the removed-selection rule reached by the user's own action. Open standalone to reach the tweaks: the initialSelection, removalPolicy, loadingTreatment and presentation policies of §05, plus a harness that slows the MES read, drops the service, has the tool room issue the die you are inspecting, and — set to accept — lets a stale response through so you can watch the panel describe a die that is no longer selected.
