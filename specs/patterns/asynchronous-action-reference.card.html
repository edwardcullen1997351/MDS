Asynchronous action

Specification §01–§21Open standalonePatterns index

Live, on Meridian’s own components: the planning console at the Chakan plant (PL-04), where an MRP regeneration runs in three stages while a planner fires availability checks at released work orders and prints a gate label. Three jobs, three latencies, one screen — each operation carries its own identity, so the answers land on their own rows and the log records the ones that were superseded or abandoned rather than losing them. Start two checks and watch the console stay usable; press a row’s check twice to meet the repeat-activation policy. Open standalone to reach the tweaks: the blocking, repeatActivation, completionFeedback, orphanPolicy, durability and feedbackDelay policies of §05, plus a harness that fails the MRP run at scheduling, fails a check, makes the answers arrive backwards, simulates a reload so a job in flight can be met as a lost record, an unknown outcome, or one re-attached by its server identity, and puts a second planner's job on a row so cross-user suppression can be seen refusing your press.
