PieChart Specification

Component: PieChart Package: components/data/PieChart.jsx Infrastructure: components/data/viz-core.js Standard: guidelines/data-visualization-spec-standard.card.html WCAG: Level AA Compliant

§01 Purpose & Domain Contract

PieChart visualizes a small number (typically 2–6, maximum 7) of mutually exclusive parts of a single whole, where angular sectors and arc lengths encode proportional percentage shares.

Domain Contract:
  • Strict Part-to-Whole Requirement: All values must represent non-negative quantities that sum to a genuine 100% total. Negative values are mathematically invalid and strictly prohibited.
  • 7-Category Ceiling: For comparing >6 categories, or when precise ranking of subtle differences is primary, do NOT use a pie chart; use a horizontal BarChart instead.
  • When to Use: Use when answering high-level composition questions (e.g. scrap distribution, energy load breakdown, downtime classification).

§06 Interactive & Visual States

  • Resting: All sectors rendered with 0.9 fill opacity + overlaid pattern preset + 2px white separator border.
  • Hover / Pointer Over: Active sector expands radially by 6px outward along its mid-angle, updating central readout and displaying tooltip.
  • Category Isolation: Clicking a legend item dims non-selected sectors to 0.20 opacity.
  • Keyboard Active: Focused sector displays 2px solid active highlight and live screen reader announcement.
  • Loading Skeleton: Circular placeholder ring maintains layout during async query.

§07 Radial Geometry Mathematics & Part-to-Whole Rules

Sector Angle & Centroid Calculation: $$\theta_i = 2\pi \times \frac{v_i}{\sum v}, \quad \theta_{mid, i} = \frac{\theta_{start, i} + \theta_{end, i}}{2}$$ $$\text{Displaced Center}: (cx_{active}, cy_{active}) = (cx + \delta \cos\theta_{mid}, cy + \delta \sin\theta_{mid})$$
Strict Input Validation:

Negative values are strictly forbidden in part-to-whole encodings. computePieSlices() automatically clamps negative values to 0 to preserve mathematical validity.

§09 Accessibility & Assistive Technology (WCAG 2.2 AA)

WCAG 1.4.1 (Non-Color Reliance) Strict Compliance:

Sector color fills must not be the sole identifier. PieChart enforces this by overlaying SVG hatch and stipple patterns (PATTERN_PRESETS), providing high-contrast separator borders, and rendering direct percentage labels.

  • ARIA Landmark: role="region", aria-roledescription="pie chart", with descriptive aria-label.
  • Live Screen Reader Region: aria-live="polite" announces: "Selected sector Main Motors: 520 kWh, representing 49.5% of total aggregate."
  • Full Tabular Parity: Built-in Alt+F11 semantic data table guarantees 100% information parity.

§10 Motion & Transition Guidelines

  • Radial Expansion Transitions: Sector displacement executes within 150ms using cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1).
  • Prefers Reduced Motion: When @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) is active, all transitions execute instantly with 0ms duration.

§11 Content Rules & Number Formatting

  • Locale Convention: Default to Indian numbering system (en-IN, e.g. ₹1,45,000, 1,050 kWh).
  • Percentage Precision: Proportions formatted to 1 decimal place (e.g. 49.5%) and guaranteed to sum to exactly 100.0%.
  • Center Readout Units: Always declare explicit engineering units in center and tooltip readouts (`kWh`, `kg`, `hrs`, `parts`).

§12 Responsive Behavior & Container Constraints

  • Direct Labels vs Legend: On narrow viewports (<340px), suppress direct arc labels and rely on the wrapped categorical legend below the chart.

§13 Anti-Patterns & Misconfigurations

Strictly Prohibited Anti-Patterns:
  1. 3D Perspective Extrusion: 3D tilted pies distort angular geometry and misrepresent proportions.
  2. Resting Exploded Slices: Permanently exploding all sectors breaks the visual perception of the circular whole.
  3. Negative Values: Never feed negative numbers into pie charts.
  4. Concentric Nested Pies: Multiple concentric rings are extremely difficult to decode; use stacked bar charts instead.
  5. Excessive Categories (>7): Never render more than 7 slices; consolidate minor categories into "Other".

§14 Implementation & Developer Notes

  • Pure React & SVG: Implemented natively in React SVG without external chart libraries, importing modular radial math from viz-core.js.
  • Clean SVG Geometry: Uses standard SVG `` with arc commands (`A`) rather than canvas bitmaps.
