Live, on Meridian’s own components: the delivery-expediting queue at the Chakan plant (PL-04) on 2026-09-08 (IST). Fourteen open purchase-order lines past their promise date — Nashik Forge brackets, Sahyadri bearings, Godavari castings, Deccan tooling — searched, filtered by supplier and status, sorted by days late and paged eight at a time. Anjali Deshmukh picks a line and the detail region names it, shows the schedule, what has been received against it, the follow-up open with the supplier, and the three actions that belong to that line: revise the promise, escalate to the supplier contact, close the line short. Selecting never moves focus out of the queue — Jump to the selected line is the deliberate way across (§07). Open standalone to reach the tweaks: the two region widths, initialSelection, selection persistence across search, filter and paging, scroll ownership, and a simulate available width switch that forces the §11 sequential form with its position-preserving return. Close a line short, or let the buyer close it from another terminal, to reach the fourth state — selected item unavailable.
