## 1. Executive Summary

The Planner Workbench reference assembly (`ra-planner-workbench`) models the mission-critical master supply chain planning cockpit connecting **Asclepius (Food Plant F-119)** and **Anantshriveda (RM Warehouse H-9)**. It integrates all Wave 1 through Wave 3 patterns:
1. **Workbench3PaneLayout**: 3-pane layout with collapsible resource tree, central time-phased matrix/dispatch board, and right-hand telemetry/order inspector.
2. **EntityFacilitySelector**: Unified facility scoping between manufacturing facilities and central raw material warehouses.
3. **TreeGridCell & TimePhasedMatrix**: High-density hierarchical grid with keyboard arrow navigation, collapsible parent rows, and cell selection.
4. **TimeHorizonStepper**: Stepped horizon buckets (Shift, Day, Week, Month, Quarter) with tabular time windows.
5. **DualUomBadge**: Primary/secondary dual unit of measure display (KG ↔ L, MT ↔ Bags).
6. **StaleDataPill**: Dynamic real-time cache freshness indicator with manual/auto refresh.
7. **ConcurrentConflictResolver**: Side-by-side drawer comparison for version conflicts (ETag 409) with 3 one-click resolution actions.
8. **OptimisticDragReschedule**: Drag-and-drop and touch/keyboard shift dispatch board with optimistic validation and smooth rebound.
9. **StockRunwayHorizon**: 24px inline micro-horizontal stock runway SVG visualization with safe zone, reorder threshold, and stockout event marker.
10. **LeadTimeTransferGlyph & IntercompanyStockRibbon**: Intercompany transfer badges and consolidated 4-metric STO balance strip.

## 3. Scenarios & Archetype Stress Cases Evaluated

1. **`ready` (Typical Content)**: Standard multi-plant baseline for Asclepius Plant F-119 showing line schedules, 3 matrix items, runway horizon, and intercompany stock strip.
2. **`minimum-content` (Minimum Content)**: Single resource work center, single matrix material row, minimal order details.
3. **`stress-high-density` (Maximum Realistic Content)**: 33 matrix rows with live runway visualizations, dual UoM badges, and full tree hierarchy.
4. **`long-labels` (Long Labels)**: Complex botanical and pharmaceutical descriptions (>150 characters), long facility names, verbose batch identifiers, verifying proper text wrapping without horizontal overflow.
5. **`missing-data` (Missing Data)**: Nullable and omitted secondary UoMs, unassigned work centers, missing batch strings, zero/unassigned transit hours, displaying graceful `—` fallbacks.
6. **`loading` (Loading State)**: Accessible skeleton placeholders (`Skeleton`) across left tree pane, center matrix, and right inspector with `role="status"` and `aria-busy="true"`.
7. **`empty-state` (Empty State)**: Canonical `EmptyState` primitives for empty resource tree, empty material matrix (with reset action), and unselected order inspector.
8. **`error-state` (Error State)**: MES gateway 503 connection error alert banner with retry trigger, order capacity breach alert, and deficit risk badge.
9. **`disabled-actions` (Disabled Actions)**: Read-only mode with disabled conflict resolution and order saving buttons (`disabled={true}` with explanatory tooltips).
10. **`concurrent-conflict`**: Simulates ETag 409 mismatch with the side-by-side resolution drawer open.
11. **`optimistic-rebound`**: Displays optimistic shift dispatch board validating line capacity and raw material transit lead times.
12. **`stale`**: Simulates 6-minute stale cached telemetry with visual warning pill and refresh trigger.
13. **`anantshriveda-warehouse`**: Central RM warehouse scope showing bulk raw material stock levels and outgoing transfers to F-119.
14. **`narrow-viewport`**: Verified across 320px (`mobile-narrow`) and 390px (`mobile`) with zero horizontal overflow and responsive tab navigation.
15. **`wide-viewport`**: Verified across 1440px desktop with balanced 3-pane workbench distribution.
