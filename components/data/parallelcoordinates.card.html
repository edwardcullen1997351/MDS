Parallel Coordinates (Multivariate Analysis & Clustering)

Compare multivariate profiles across many quantitative dimensions to identify clustering, trade-offs, Pareto frontiers, and anomalous manufacturing records. Features axis reordering, normalized 0–100% scale, smooth cubic spline curves, accessible 1D range brush filtering, hover crosshairs, and accessible data table (Alt+F11).

1. Die Casting & Stamping Multi-Parameter Telemetry (Chakan PL-04)
5-Dimensional telemetry comparing Peak Tonnage, Die Temp, Injection Pressure, Cycle Time, and Defect PPM with interactive brush filters and axis reordering.
2. Steel Coil Batch Metallurgy & Mechanical Properties
6-Dimensional material testing across Tensile Strength, Yield Strength, Elongation, Hardness, Carbon Equivalent, and Surface Roughness.
3. CNC 5-Axis Spindle Telemetry & Tool Wear Correlation (Smooth Spline)
Smooth cubic spline polylines with normalized scale comparing Spindle Load %, Tool Wear, Vibration, and Surface Finish.
