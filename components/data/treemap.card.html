Treemap (Hierarchy & Part-to-Whole)

Nested rectangular area visualization preserving parent-child hierarchy containment. Features squarified & slice-and-dice layouts, interactive drilldown breadcrumbs, adaptive label suppression, WCAG non-color hatch patterns, and accessible tabular tree modals.

1. Plant Power Consumption Hierarchy (Chakan Plant PL-04)
3-level hierarchy: Plant Section → Shop Subsystem → Machine Load. Click on any section or press Enter to drill down.
2. Scrap Material Cost Loss Breakdown (INR)
Raw material grades (CRCA Steel, Aluminum 6061, Stainless Steel, Copper) subdivided by shop scrap mode.
3. Annual Asset Maintenance Budget Allocation (₹ Lakhs)
Plant divisions subdivided into mechanical, electrical, and tooling asset classes with accessible monochrome hatch patterns.
