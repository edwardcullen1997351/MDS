Range Chart (Bounded Intervals & Uncertainty)

Compare bounded intervals, statistical confidence, before/after endpoints, process variation, and tolerance deviations. Features floating interval bars, dumbbell delta links, error whiskers, continuous range area bands, WCAG 1.4.1 hatch fills, and accessible data table (Alt+F11).

1. CNC Machining Critical Tolerance Bands (Interval Bar Variant)
Floating range bars showing Lower & Upper Spec Limits (LSL / USL) with central measured dimension ticks and nominal target line.
2. Shop-Floor Kaizen Cycle Time Reduction (Dumbbell Variant)
Before vs After endpoint comparison highlighting cycle time optimization (green) vs process bottlenecks (red).
3. Steel Coil Tensile Yield Strength (95% CI Error Bar Variant)
Central batch mean with 95% Confidence Interval error whiskers across incoming raw material heats.
4. Furnace Heat Cycle Temperature Variation (Range Area Band)
Continuous sequence envelope displaying min/max temperature variation band with central thermocouple telemetry.
