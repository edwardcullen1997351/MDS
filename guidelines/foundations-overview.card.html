Foundations Master Overview

The Meridian Design System visual foundation is documented across 51 guideline cards. This master overview indexes every foundation domain into a structured taxonomy for rapid wayfinding and onboarding.

1. Visual Foundations Taxonomy

7 Core Domains

1. Color & Theming (7 cards)

  • Ramps: Grey scale, blue action, semantic state hues (green, orange, red, teal).
  • Semantics: Text roles (primary, secondary, tertiary, disabled), surfaces, borders.
  • Dark Scope: Multi-theme token overrides with alpha compositing.
  • Data Viz: --viz-1…6 series sequence.

2. Typography & Numerals (8 cards)

  • Typefaces: Inter (sans) for prose/labels, JetBrains Mono for codes/identifiers.
  • Tabular Figures: tabular-nums on all numeric data columns.
  • Scale: --text-2xs (10px) to --text-4xl (32px).
  • Hierarchy: Strict heading levels independent of visual size.

3. Geometry & Spacing (6 cards)

  • 4px Base Grid: --space-1 (4px) to --space-16 (64px).
  • Control Heights: Standardized 28px / 34px / 44px rhythm.
  • Row Rhythm: 36px (compact), 44px (comfortable), 56px (expanded).

4. Radius & Hairlines (6 cards)

  • Radius Scale: --radius-xs (2px) to --radius-full (9999px).
  • Structural Borders: Hairlines (1px) carry architecture; 0 ordinals.
  • Control Boundaries: --border-control for 3:1 WCAG AA compliance.

5. Elevation & Layers (5 cards)

  • Z-Index Ladder: Strict 6-level stacking ladder (0 to 500).
  • Shadow Roles: --elevation-0 to --elevation-5.
  • Focus Rings: Inset / outline focus rules preventing container clipping.

6. Motion & Choreography (5 cards)

  • 3-Loop Budget: mer-spin, mer-indeterminate, mer-skeleton-sweep.
  • Durations: Instant (50ms), Fast (120ms), Base (180ms), Slow (240ms).
  • A11y: Instantaneous 1ms collapse under prefers-reduced-motion.
